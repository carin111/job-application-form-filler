# Targeting rules

Use role relevance to select and order experiences. These are defaults, not permission to invent missing experience.

## Operations / ecommerce / marketing / brand

Prioritize experiences demonstrating campaign execution, conversion or funnel analysis, GMV/ROI awareness, content operations, supplier or brand coordination, user lifecycle work, data monitoring, and cross-team delivery.

## Product

Prioritize user research or feedback, requirement extraction, process design, prototyping, product iteration, data diagnosis, experimentation, and stakeholder coordination.

## Data / business analysis

Prioritize data cleaning, metric frameworks, SQL or spreadsheet analysis, dashboards, experimentation, anomaly diagnosis, insights, and decisions influenced. Distinguish analysis performed from business outcomes owned.

## Finance / banking / securities / insurance / investment

Prioritize industry research, financial analysis, audit or compliance work, investment or transaction support, databases, models, reports, and regulated-process discipline.

## Consulting / strategy / research

Prioritize structured problem solving, analytical frameworks, primary or secondary research, data synthesis, reports, presentations, and recommendations.

## State-owned enterprise / comprehensive management

Prioritize research and writing, organization and coordination, compliance, reliable delivery, leadership or service, relevant awards, and evidence of long-term fit. Avoid inflated strategic language.

## English applications

Translate all applicant-facing content into natural business English. Keep facts and dates unchanged. Use an organization's official English name only when verified; otherwise retain the original name or a transparent transliteration.

## Tailoring rules

1. Keep the canonical bank unchanged; tailor only the application copy.
2. Move the most relevant verified action and result to the beginning.
3. Reuse JD terminology only when it accurately describes the candidate's work.
4. Preserve metrics with their original context and denominator.
5. Do not upgrade “assisted” into “owned,” “led,” or “developed.”
6. If space is limited, remove the least relevant experience before stripping all detail from the strongest experience.
7. Include awards and projects when the form provides dedicated fields and they strengthen the target narrative.

## Self-description pattern

Build a short summary from verified facts:

`<DOMAIN FOUNDATION> + <2–3 RELEVANT EXPERIENCE TYPES> + <VERIFIED TOOLS> + <WORKING STYLE> + <TARGET ROLE FIT>`

Avoid generic claims that cannot be supported by the fact bank.
